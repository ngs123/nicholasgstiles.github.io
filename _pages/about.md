I am a second-year PhD student in the [Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science](https://www.eecs.mit.edu/) at MIT. I have joint positions in [RLE](https://www.rle.mit.edu/) and [LIDS](https://lids.mit.edu/) and am fortunate to be co-advised by Professors [Gregory W. Wornell](https://web.mit.edu/gww/www/) and [Yury Polyanskiy](https://people.lids.mit.edu/yp/homepage/).

My research interests lie broadly at the intersections of artificial intelligence, information theory, statistical inference, and signal processing. I'm interested in developing new, scalable approaches to classical inference problems and establishing rigorous statistical guarantees for these algorithms. In my first year as a grad student, I worked on blind inverse problems and representation learning for communication systems, and these remain active areas of research for me.
